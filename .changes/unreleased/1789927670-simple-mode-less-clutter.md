---
type: Changed
title: Simple mode is leaner — less to read, nothing to decide
---

Simple mode now says only what you need to click Next: the welcome page drops the catalog inventory and the links to the old downloadable installers, the target step replaces the list of defaults with one line, and the review step shows a short summary and a single download size instead of the per-package file breakdown. Installing shows the percentage, one bar and the files going past. Advanced mode is unchanged and still spells out every package, port and option.
