type: Changed
title: The wordmark and the step bar stay on screen while you scroll
---

On a phone the header scrolled away two flicks into the first step, taking both the "nQuake" wordmark and the "Step 2 of 6" bar with it — so the one thing that says how much of this is left was only visible at the top of a page you spend your time at the bottom of. Both now sit in a sticky bar at the top of the page, and the bar compacts as soon as you scroll: the Playball slogan steps out and the wordmark drops a size, so keeping them costs the step about one line. Desktop keeps its step list in the sidebar, now hanging below the same sticky header. The browser's own chrome is themed with it: Safari tints its address bar (and, on a phone, the toolbar at the bottom) with the page's `theme-color`, which was a colour from no part of this design — it is now the surface the Next button's bar is drawn on, so the two meet without a seam, and it follows the header's dark/light toggle rather than only the OS setting.
