type: Changed
title: Next points at the player name instead of going dead
---

The nickname field is now "Player name", with a red asterisk for the one answer the installer will not move past and without the "The name other players will see" line above it — the label says that. Pressing Next with it empty used to do nothing at all, because the button was disabled: no message, no movement, and on a phone the field it was waiting for was usually off screen. Next now stays live for it, and pressing it brings the field to the middle of the screen, puts the cursor in it and turns it red with "Enter a player name to continue." Everything else a step can be missing — a folder nobody has picked, a catalog still loading — keeps Next disabled as before, because there is nothing on the step to point at.
