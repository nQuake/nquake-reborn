type: Changed
title: The detected operating system is offered first, with a glyph on each button
---

The platform row always listed Windows, Linux, macOS in that order, so a macOS visitor on a phone found the button already chosen for them on the second line, under the two that were not. The detected OS now comes first, and each button carries its own mark — the Windows panes, an apple, a penguin — which is quicker to find than three words of the same length. The Play / Host / Both cards put their title on the same line as their icon and lost a little padding: stacked, the three of them filled a phone screen on their own and pushed the platform and player name below the fold. The Simple target step also drops the paragraph telling you to switch to Advanced; the switch itself is in the card header, directly above it.
