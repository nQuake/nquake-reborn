---
type: Changed
title: Choosing the QRP textures leaves out the 24-bit pack they replace
---

The high-resolution QRP textures override the standard 24-bit world textures, so installing both only downloaded a pack that never showed. With QRP on, the 24-bit toggle now reads off and is disabled, and the install skips it; turning QRP back off restores your earlier choice. The Advanced target step also drops its paragraph describing what the next steps hold — Simple has none, and the steps speak for themselves.
