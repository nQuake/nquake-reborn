title: The wizard card no longer stretches to the bottom of the window on desktop
---

On a desktop screen every step's card was pulled down to the bottom of the window, so a short step like "What to install" left a large empty area between its fields and the Next button. The card now fits its content, with Back / Next right below the last field. The Review step also explains why a browser install shows only the folder's name (`nq`) rather than its full path (`~/nq`): the browser's folder access gives the page the name and nothing else. The desktop app still shows the full path.
